## What is new in 0.3.4

- **Show me around** is in the sidebar of every view. The tour now goes from **Find** to **Profile**, through the five steps of the profile wizard, and on to **Settings**. It saves nothing, and it puts you back where you started when it ends.
- Each box in the profile wizard has a question mark that says what it is for, and each text box shows sample text until you type.
- The wizard says what an organization that applies in its own name enters under **Name**, **Institution** and **Career stage**.
- Step 3 of the wizard, **Your terms**, opens with an empty table when the draft has no terms yet.
- The wizard proposes the words and phrases that your own text repeats. It no longer proposes general words such as "we" and "far".
- A **Keyword** is sent to the NIH Guide as an exact phrase, so "chronic pain" finds the notices that hold that phrase. Put a comma between two phrases to search for either.
- The documentation has a new walk-through, from the first profile to an exported application, with a nonprofit as the example.
