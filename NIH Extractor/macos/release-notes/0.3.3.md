## What is new in 0.3.3

- The sidebar is shorter. **Keyword** and **Run Search** stay in view, and **Source and rows**, **Display** and **Export** open when you select them.
- **Show me around** is at the top of the sidebar, under **View**.
- The **Database** path moved to **Settings**.
- The tour no longer plays the alert sound when you press Escape or the left or right arrow key. Its Details step points at the tabs, and it follows the light or dark theme.
- The **Model** section links to the page where Claude, OpenAI and Gemini each give out an API key, and its list holds the models that can write text.
- **Test connection** gives up after a minute without an answer, and says what a failure means.
- Grant Matcher checks for a new release each time it opens, and this window shows what changed.
