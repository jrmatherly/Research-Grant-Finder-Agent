## What is new in 0.3.5

- **Apply** shows Section II, the award information, and Section III, the eligibility information, of a fetched announcement. Read Section III before you write. Grant Matcher shows it and does not check it.
- A model's polish keeps the paragraphs of the section. The struck and bold words used to run together as one paragraph. A struck ¶ marks a paragraph break that the polish removes.
- The profile wizard proposes a hyphenated word such as "energy-limiting" or "state-of-the-art" whole. It no longer proposes a half such as "self" or "energy", which matched every word that starts with it.
